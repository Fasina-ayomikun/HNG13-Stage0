About Me

Bio

Hi, I’m Fasina Ayomikun, a frontend developer and embedded systems enthusiast who loves turning ideas into clean, user-friendly digital experiences. With over four years of hands-on experience working with React, Next.js, and modern web technologies, I enjoy simplifying complex concepts and creating beautiful interfaces that actually solve problems.

Beyond coding, I’m a content creator and the founder of Tech with Deeyah, where I share my frontend development journey. I believe technology should be inclusive, inspiring, and empowering, that’s what drives me every day.

Goals in This Program

My primary goal in this program is to sharpen my frontend development skills, master scalable UI architecture, and strengthen my collaboration abilities by working with other developers across diverse projects.

I also want to improve how I plan, test, and optimize code for accessibility and performance, building not just beautiful websites but usable and inclusive ones.

Areas of Low Confidence

I’m still growing my confidence in advanced backend concepts, especially when it comes to handling server logic, APIs, and database optimization. I sometimes overthink design decisions or spend too much time perfecting layouts, learning to balance functionality over perfection is something I’m actively working on.

Note to Future Self

Keep learning, but don’t forget to pause and celebrate progress. You’ve come a long way, from debugging your first “hello world” to building full web applications that make a difference. Stay curious, stay humble, and never let self-doubt win. You are becoming the kind of engineer you once looked up to.

Extra Thoughts

I’m grateful for communities like HNG that create spaces for growth, learning, and real collaboration. This journey isn’t just about code; it’s about people, persistence, and purpose. My goal is to keep using tech to create impact, tell stories, and help others find their path in this amazing field.
